# Week 1 Reflections
This will contain my reflections from week 1 of the Software Engineering module

Software is a compilation of instructions for a computer to follow to do a specific task/tasks
Engineering is the application of maths, science and designs to improve and solve real-world problems.
In computing and software, engineering is the application of certain methodologies, standards and practices into the development, testing and maintaining of software
A piece of software that has typically worked well for me is Obsidian; which is a high-level text editor designed for taking and keeping track of notes with a very intuitive and easy to understand UI and also supports markdown features/formatting and has extensive support for community developed plug-ins.
A piece of software that has frustrated me is the Epic Games Launcher; which is a launcher a store front for many games and software, however it has a very unintuitive and unpleasing UI, very poor performance where it takes up way more system resources than it should, constant pop-ups and occasionally switches off dark-mode by itself.
